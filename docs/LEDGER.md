# Virtual accounts and authoritative ledger

Implemented experimental P3-02 native Rust library. [Project status](PROJECT_STATUS.md) owns task closure. `fqe_runtime::ledger::VirtualLedger` is the authoritative book for one local account, one instrument and one currency. Construct independent ledgers for separate accounts; no global account registry, FX conversion, persistence service, network, login or SDK is required. [Financial/history](FINANCIAL_HISTORY.md) owns exact units. [Simulation](SIMULATION.md) and [Delivery plan](DELIVERY_PLAN.md) own remaining execution/risk/report gates.

## Profiles and action boundary

AccountConfig freezes account ID, validated FinancialInstrument, initial nonnegative cash and an explicit LedgerProfile. All profiles require nonnegative-price instrument metadata and strictly positive trade/mark prices. Neither accepts signed-price financial execution. API decimals are implicitly in this account's declared currency or base-quantity unit; no alternate currency is inferred or converted.

| Profile | Supported accounting | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fqe.gross-basis-cash-long/1 | Nonnegative cash/long inventory, gross money basis, fees separately expensed, proportional partial close | Cannot sell beyond current long position |
| fqe.gross-basis-margin-long/1 | Gross-basis long inventory plus balanced Borrow/Repay principal and explicit debt | [Margin broker profile](MARGIN_PROFILES.md) owns risk; financing alone does not enforce leverage |
| fqe.gross-basis-restricted-short/1 | Signed inventory/cost, gross opening basis, short sale proceeds restricted from spending/withdrawal; closing releases proportional proceeds | Accounting-only short profile; no collateral adequacy, borrow, maintenance, leverage, liquidation or broker admission promise |

These profiles are deliberately different from Desk's fee-capitalized Decimal34 book. A single trade cannot close a position and cross zero into the opposite side: explicit close and open postings are required. Transfers, Reserve, Release and Trade are typed trusted-host accounting commands; a Trade is a supplied execution posting, never a signal, order intent, generated fill or live confirmation. P3-03 owns intent policy, order lifecycle, timing, price selection, liquidity, minimum-notional/order-size admission and fee-rate computation. The experimental [P3-04 margin profile](MARGIN_PROFILES.md) owns supported broker margin/risk and subsequent-open liquidation; other models remain unsupported. A short account can report negative marked equity while a cover beyond available cash is rejected; this is not automatic liquidation or a claim that short losses are bounded.

## Balanced authoritative journal

Creation always records sequence1/opening at timestamp0, including zero initial cash. Opening binds the full AccountConfig and exact engine source identity. Each subsequent LedgerTransaction has a valid unique ID, nondecreasing timestamp and monotonically assigned journal sequence. Failed IDs remain retryable; successful IDs cannot be reused. Equal timestamps are ordered by journal sequence.

MoneyPostings use one declared money scale/step and accounts Cash, InventoryCost, ExternalCapital, RealizedProfit, FeeExpense and LoanDebt. Every entry sums to zero independently. QuantityPostings debit inventory and credit its external counterparty by equal opposite signed base quantities. Reserve/release entries carry no financial movement; their exact command and resulting held/free views are journaled. Private cached balances are updated only together with the journal receipt after validation; consumers cannot edit them through getters.

Positive deposit/withdrawal changes cash and external capital with opposite postings. Trade gross is the exact checked price×quantity, settled to the actual money step with HalfAwayFromZero. Supplied fee must already be a nonnegative money-step amount. Buy/sell cash flow is minus/plus settled gross minus fee. Open inventory basis uses settled gross, with fees posted separately. Closing basis is proportional to the original remaining gross cost, half-even to a money step; the final close sweeps all residual basis. Realized gross is sale gross minus allocated long basis, or allocated short proceeds minus cover gross. Quantity lots are validated independently of order minimum size so a partial fill/residual may be smaller than a minimum order. Order admission must still enforce that minimum separately.

TradeAudit records exact gross, settled gross, allocated basis and signed cash-rounding residual (actual cash flow minus its unrounded equivalent). The cumulative residual is exact at scale max(18, price_scale + quantity_scale), up to20 and is diagnostic, not an extra cash movement. Proportional basis is explicitly rounded; it may differ from Desk's Decimal34 allocation. All coefficients/products/additions are bounded/checked; bounds originate in FinancialInstrument, and zero-position cost must be zero.

Every committed state satisfies `cash + signed_cost - debt = external_capital + realized_gross - fees`. Views derive reserved cash/quantity, restricted short proceeds, available cash and available long quantity. `value_at` takes a caller-supplied positive tick-valid mark, uses signed position and the same gross rounding, and returns market value/equity/unrealized/total profit without changing any journal. `equity - external_capital = realized_gross + unrealized - fees`, with equity cash+market value-debt. MarginLong alone also accepts zero marks. These are accounting formulas, not performance/risk metrics or independently sourced marks.

## Reservations, atomicity and limits

A nonempty reservation has a unique lifetime ID and explicit nonnegative cash/long quantities. Active holds cannot exceed cash net of restricted short proceeds or existing long inventory. Short proceeds are the magnitude of remaining signed short cost; that amount remains unavailable until proportional covering. Reservation IDs cannot be reused after release. This is ownership within one trusted account API, not a multi-user permission system.

A Trade may explicitly release part or all of one named reservation atomically. Release cannot exceed its held amounts; zeroed holds are removed. Other reservations remain intact. Any remaining holds/restrictions must remain covered after the trade. Releasing a reservation does not create cash. A caller may use previously free cash as well as the released hold; model-specific order limits remain P3-03.

Maximum32768 journal entries including opening and1024 active reservations. Identifier bounds come from the shared model contract. Admission reserves journal space and checks duplicate/time conditions first, computes a candidate state, validates money/lot/range/holds/conservation/posting sums, then commits state, ID and receipt together. Invalid tick/lot/scale/fee, insufficient funds/inventory, overflow/range, reservation/time/ID/profile/capacity failures leave every balance, hold and journal entry unchanged. No provider/external callbacks or IO occur in the transaction. This is a bounded synchronous library, not a durable concurrent database or measured production-throughput service.

## Recompute replay and compatibility

`VirtualLedger::replay(config, journal)` creates a separate new candidate, requires an exact opening config/engine pin and recomputes every action. It compares the entire generated JournalEntry: sequence/transaction/postings/quantity/audit/after-view. Any mismatch, invalid action, absent opening, capacity issue or changed source identity rejects restore. Old account objects remain untouched; continuation from a verified prefix gives the same journal suffix and balances. Tests alter account/profile/engine/sequence/cash/posting/quantity/audit/duplicate data and exercise every split through funding/reserves/partial fills/releases.

JournalEntry/AccountConfig are native Rust DTOs, with no portable serialization or public ABI promise. There is no ledger file writer, external append acknowledgement, financial checkpoint integration, schema migration or stable wire format yet. P3-07/P4 must bind complete financial manifests and financial restore/durability before full backtest/app claims. Any engine source change invalidates this same-engine opening pin deliberately.

## Independent comparison and verification

`tests/reference/p3_ledger_oracle.py --check` uses Python stdlib Fraction over five hand-defined public synthetic cases and verifies16 frozen rows in [ledger expectations](../crates/fqe-runtime/tests/fixtures/p3/ledger.expected.tsv). It never imports/executes Rust or Desk. Expected files are never rewritten by checks; only explicit authoring --write updates them. The script also verifies the P3-01 cash-long comparison baseline. Rust compares each row, reconciles all money/quantity postings independently and verifies prefix replay.

Cash-long initial1000, buy2 at120 with0.50 fee, sell2 at130 with0.50 fee gives759.50 then1019 cash and19 net in both FQE and the independently expressed legacy arithmetic. After buy, FQE gross cost240 and separately expensed0.50 differ from legacy fee-capitalized240.50. FQE realized gross20/fees1 differ from legacy realized net19. All supported long fixture cash rows and flat net results are compared with the legacy Fraction columns; intermediate proportional cost/unrealized classification is not claimed equal. The rational legacy model expresses selected inspected Desk equations and is not execution or Decimal34 conformance of the actual dirty Desk tree. Actual Desk backend parity/migration remains P4.

Other fixtures cover multiple long lots and partial basis, short3@100/cover1@90/cover2@110 (cash988, realized gross-10, fees2), cash half ties and basis half-even/final sweep. Additional tests cover actual0.05 money steps, maximum balances/quantities, below-minimum partial lots, named reservations, insufficient funds, restricted proceeds/negative marked equity, unique/time conditions,32768/1024 capacity and atomic rejection. `cargo run --locked --offline -p fqe-runtime --example virtual_ledger` reads the public historical fixture and posts two explicitly supplied trades, prints3 journal entries/cash1019/fees1/net19 and verifies replay. It does not perform matching or strategy-to-order conversion.

No new dependencies/manifest/lockfile/toolchain changes, packages, scanner uploads or Desk writes/execution are required. Full existing P1/P2 regression remains required. Multi-currency/netting, signed-price fills, margin/liquidation, corporate actions/tax, live routing, unbounded history/durable append, report/optimizer and actual Desk integration are not delivered by this slice.
