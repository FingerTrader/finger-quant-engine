# Linear shared-margin research profile

Implemented experimental scope, 2026-11-15. Owner-requested Desk strategy parameters: leverage and maximum per-entry percentage of current account equity. Profile `fqe.linear-shared-margin-open/1` is separate from the existing fully cash-collateralized research profile and the existing financed long profile. P4-03.03 remains IN_PROGRESS; this does not close a phase or add a live broker capability.

## Inputs and ordering

Research context explicitly selects `capitalModel: linear_margin`, `accountMode: margin`, integer `leverage` 1..1000 and decimal `entryPercent` greater than0 through100. All values are strings. Native capabilities publish `linear_margin`, `margin_profile` and `max_leverage`. Unsupported helpers must reject before execution. Omitted/cash context preserves old execution and strict legacy input handling. Base-unit quantity and a single shared quote currency are required; contract multipliers, inverse settlement, FX, funding rates, interest and venue-specific margin tiers are outside this experimental model.

Each accepted entry freezes a money-step-rounded-down budget = max(current equity,0) × percent /100. Existing pending entries reserve their budgets; used marked margin and pending holds reduce available margin. At a later eligible open, the engine uses the smaller of remaining frozen budget, percentage of then-current equity, and available margin including this order's own hold. It chooses the largest quantity-step-aligned quantity (up to the existing1,000,000-unit research bound) whose conservatively rounded initial margin plus actual notional-based fees fits that budget. Entry-node fixed quantity/cashCap/dynamic quantity do not participate in this explicit mode; other action and protection semantics remain unchanged. FOK cannot partially fill after resizing. Quantity zero/below minimum is rejected truthfully. Liquidity uses the existing prior-closed-bar policy; no future high/low/close participates in a fill.

Initial margin = ceil(marked gross exposure / leverage) at account money step. Sizing first rounds gross exposure upward to avoid spending an extra sub-money-step amount. Fees retain the instrument's existing rounded gross fee rule. P&L is actual price movement × actual quantity, never multiplied by leverage a second time. Closing/reducing releases exposure margin. Each later entry recomputes its budget from equity, rather than original initial capital. Same-symbol open marks are updated before execution; other symbols keep their latest known marks.

## Accounting and liquidation

VirtualLedger remains the posting authority. Existing gross-basis long/short legs use explicitly journaled internal clearing credit from the hub; hub debt is subtracted from total equity. Credit is repaid from unreserved settlement cash. This is an internal simulation posting mechanism, not external funding, customer borrowing or profit. Reserved short proceeds cannot fund risk beyond the shared-margin admission rules. Report margin fields expose settled balance (initial capital + realized gross P&L − fees), used margin, available margin, leverage, percent and status. Curve cash means settled balance for this profile. Each replay frame carries its own margin snapshot.

This profile's explicit experimental maintenance rule is50% of marked initial-margin requirement. At close, equity below maintenance cancels pending user orders and queues reduce-only system market liquidations for subsequent eligible opens. System liquidation cannot be cancelled by a strategy; small residual quantities may close below entry minimum. New entries remain blocked during/after liquidation. No future bar is invented, zero liquidity does not pretend to close, and end-of-input does not auto-settle positions. A gap can leave negative equity and unpaid clearing debt, reported as insolvent; it is never clamped to zero. Quota and response limits still apply.
